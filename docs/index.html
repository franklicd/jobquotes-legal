Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Information We Collect

JobSiteQuotes is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter — including company information, quotes, client details, photos, and calendar events — is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any user data
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services
  • We do not share any information with third parties

Data Access Permissions

The app requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera — To take job site photos and attach them to quotes
  • Photo Library — To select existing photos and attach them to quotes
  • Location — To search for nearby addresses when filling in client information
  • Calendar — To add quote reminders and schedule events

These permissions are used solely for the stated purposes. You can revoke any permission at any time in your device Settings.

Data You Share

When you use the PDF sharing or CSV export features, the generated files contain only the information you have entered. You control who receives these files. We never see or transmit them.

In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive a transaction receipt but do not receive your full payment information (credit card number, etc.). Apple's privacy policy applies to payment processing.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
